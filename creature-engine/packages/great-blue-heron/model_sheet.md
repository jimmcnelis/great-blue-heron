# Great Blue Heron — model sheet (v1, transcribed from the built rig)

Construction facts. Change one of these and every canonical pose gets
re-rendered and re-checked (cartoon-craft skill: a proportion change IS a
model-sheet change).

## Proportions (design space 120×90; from the real bird, per Cornell Lab)

- Standing height ≈ 4 design units per foot of real bird; legs ≈ **40% of
  standing height** (Jimmy: real heron legs are "comically long" — honor it).
- Bill length ≈ shin length (dagger beak, ~13.5 design units).
- Body: teardrop ellipse 33 × 15.2, fuller at the rear-top.
- Stance (Jimmy's v1.1 ruling): body pitched to a **30–40° diagonal**, not
  horizontal; strike coils FROM the diagonal.
- Neck: tapered Bézier tube 6.6 → 4.8 wide; upright S, coiled hunting S, and
  full extension are the three canonical neck poses. In flight the neck folds
  into a tight S, head drawn back to the shoulders (v1 weakness: untucked —
  v1.2 brief item).
- Legs: stilt-thin 3-segment IK (rusty thigh 9, shin 13.5, tarsus 15.5).
  Flight: legs trail straight behind the tail as thin near-parallel lines
  (v1.1 ruling: legs too thick, especially in flight).

## Canonical marks (may never change shape)

- White face; black brow band + trailing plume (2 strands).
- Black shoulder patch at the neck/body junction.
- Rusty thighs; dark-brown lower legs.
- Orange dagger beak; pale neck with chest-fringe zigzag.
- Folded wing: dark-slate scalloped feathers over slate body.
