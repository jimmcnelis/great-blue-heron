# Pond Frog — model sheet (plug-test species; deliberately simple)

- Sits on a shore line (design y=52), body centre baseline y=44.
- Body 22 × 14 ellipse; head merged at front-top; two eye bumps that must
  stay ≥2 cells at final grid or the frog loses its face.
- Vocal sac under the mouth: the croak is told by the sac, not the mouth.
- Hind legs folded at the sides; mid-hop they extend back-down (never
  straight — a frog's hop reads in the fold).
- Tongue: one straight fast line + sticky tip; out and back inside 0.4 s.
