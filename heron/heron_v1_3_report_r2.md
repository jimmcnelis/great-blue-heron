## What changed, debt by debt

1. **Flight — redrawn from the footage.** The old wing was a tapered tube along a two-bone spine (max width 6.2, tip 0.9) — that's why it read as fins. The wing is now an authored closed outline: deep chord (~10 u), a leading edge root→wrist→tip, and a **fingered trailing edge** (primary notches measured off clip-05's frontal braking fan and clip-06's mantle dome), drawn as layered surfaces — covert rows over a dark base, a deep-tone primary zone at the trailing edge; the far wing is the same outline, smaller, darker, rotated further back. The flight torso also keeps its feather structure now: scallop rows lie along the back inside the torso loop instead of the plain oval. The flight silhouette (new audit: 40,265 px) names "heron in flight" on its own.
2. **Front stop — redrawn per clip-01's front frames.** New FRONT_TORSO: narrow and tall (max half-width 9.6 → 7.2), widest at the folded-wing sides, tapering below. The two dark ovals are gone; in their place, **vertical folded-wing side panels** (with scallop hints in naturalist), a **centred breast plume column** continuous with the throat beard, a ragged belly skirt, legs close. No ball, no totem.
3. **Swallow bulge — legible at full frame.** Pose data untouched; the render changed: width factor 1.25 → 1.8, gaussian σ 0.17 → 0.22, and the lump now carries tone, not just silhouette — a two-step shadow slice (SLATE halo, DARKSLATE core) crossing the light throat band plus a pale cap on the nape side, both riding the lump. Eye-verified at full 1024×600: the lump visibly descends f406 → f412 → f418. It reads as a big fish going down, not as a cartoon.
4. **Storybook beard — restored to C scale.** Three attempts at an offset-polygon mass collapsed into ribbons (the throat runs nearly parallel to any downward offset, so a band between throat and tips-line has ~zero width — measured, not guessed). The shipped construction is the naturalist mane's own mechanism restyled: nine FAT flat strands (w0 2.6 vs 0.6), one PALE tone, whose overlap is the mass and whose tips are the ragged hem. It hangs the same depth as the naturalist beard in every pose — rest, coil, strike (streamed along the neck), front, and flight (pressed back). The style comparison is now fair: same beard, two renderings.
5. **Shoulder patch — feathered into the rows.** It was a hard 4-point quad (and a Catmull-Rom pass melted its replacement into a round button — caught on my own frame pull). Final form: an elongated feather-flow wedge drawn as a RAW polygon (no spline) with a stepped scalloped lower edge, the rusty speck overlapping its top, and DEEP scallop tips overlapping its lower edge so it emerges from the feather mass. In the strike frames it now sits in the rows instead of floating on them.
