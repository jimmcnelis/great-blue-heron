# Heron device — the 480 fork, researched 2026-11-02

Prepared proactively after Jimmy downshifted the screen from 720 to ~480 and raised color e-ink.
No parts bought. Double-confirmation rule applies before any order.

## The headline

"480" lands on **800×480** — and, coincidentally, the Spectra 6 e-ink panel Jimmy
considered and scratched is also exactly 800×480. The live pick is now between that
800×480 ESP32-S3 route and a 1024×600 ESP32-P4 route; the grid consequences of each
are spelled out below, because they are the real difference between the two.

## Fork A — LCD (the living bird)

**Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-4.3B** — a single board that is screen + brain in one:

- 4.3" IPS LCD, 800×480, 65K color, 5-point capacitive touch
- ESP32-S3 (N16R8: 16 MB flash, 8 MB PSRAM) — the same chip family as the BOX-3 / Skunkflame,
  so the on-device generation pattern Jimmy already owns transfers directly
- USB-C power, microSD slot, LiPo charging circuit on board
- Board outline 112.4 × 75.1 mm — a small desk frame; a printed case is a few dollars of PETG
- Price: US$29.99 at launch (Notebookcheck); currently listed ~$37–44 at EU/UK retailers,
  ~$25–33 via marketplace sellers. Also sold as a -BOX version with a plastic case.
- A 5" sibling (same 800×480) lists around $38–40 if 4.3" reads too small on a shelf.

Why it fits: fluid motion (flight, strike, swallow all play as animation), full storybook
palette exactly as drawn, instant-on, ~1–2 W class draw dominated by the backlight.
The touch panel even makes the "fish now" button free — tap the water.

**Prototype BOM, LCD route:** board $30–40 + USB-C cable/supply $5–8 + printed case ~$4 +
screws/standoffs ~$3 ≈ **$42–55 all-in** — versus $85–120 for the 720/Pi route sketched
earlier, and no Pi, no SD-card Linux image, no boot screen to hide.

## Fork B — ESP32-P4, 7″ 1024×600 (the bigger bird) — added after Jimmy's pick narrowed

Researched after the live pick became S3-480 vs P4-600 (e-ink scratched, see below).

**Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Touch-LCD-7B:**

- 7" IPS, 1024×600, GT911 5-point capacitive touch, 350 cd/m², 160° viewing angle
- ESP32-P4: RISC-V dual-core up to 360 MHz + LP core, 32 MB PSRAM, 32 MB flash;
  Wi-Fi 6 / BT 5 via an ESP32-C6 coprocessor; USB-C power/programming; microSD
- Display area 154.6 × 86.4 mm; board 164.3 × 99.2 mm — true shelf/picture presence,
  vs the 4.3" S3 board's 112 × 75 mm desk-trinket footprint
- Price: US$51.99 in stock (Sunsky, basic version); the camera version is €67.24 (Kamami)
- **Prototype BOM, P4 route:** board $52 + printed case ~$6 (bigger shell) + power $6
  + hardware $3 ≈ **$64–70** — still far under the 720/Pi sketch, and the 7" size
  supports the top of the $99–149 retail band or a step above it

Two honest costs:

1. **Grid math.** The sim's palette grid at 800×480 is 160×96 at an exact ×5 integer
   scale — every cell a perfect square block, zero shimmer. At 1024×600 that grid has
   no integer scale (×6.4 / ×6.25); going P4 means either a redesigned grid
   (e.g. 256×150 at ×4 — ~2.7× the cells, real retraining cost on the Spark) or
   non-integer scaling, which shimmers on flat storybook shapes. Not free, not fatal —
   but it is work the S3 route doesn't need.
2. **Chip maturity.** Waveshare's own docs warn the P4 "currently has limited adaptation
   on the Arduino platform" and steer developers to ESP-IDF; the P4 is new silicon
   (some boards still ship the original v1 chipset rather than the newer revision).
   The S3, by contrast, is the exact chip family already running Skunkflame in the
   house — known toolchain, known traps.

## Fork C — Color e-ink, Spectra 6 — SCRATCHED BY JIMMY 2026-11-02, do not revive

Kept here only as the record of why it died: it fit the paper/ink aesthetic and drew
~zero power while still, but the seconds-long color refresh killed fluid motion.

**7.3" E Ink Spectra 6 panel — also exactly 800×480**, active area 160 × 96 mm,
panel outline 170.2 × 111.2 × 0.91 mm:

- 6 colors only: black, white, red, green, blue, yellow. No partial refresh.
- Full refresh 12 s nominal; Pimoroni's real-world figure for current panels is a
  20–35 s complete update cycle (28 s core refresh on panels made from April 2026).
- Power only while refreshing (<70 mW); the image persists with power removed. No backlight.
- Prices: Seeed bare panel $45; Waveshare 7.3" (E) module ~$55–62; Pimoroni Inky Frame 7.3"
  (Pico 2 W, finished frame) $99.75; Waveshare's finished wood-frame ESP32-S3 PhotoPainter
  ~€101 retail.

Three honest problems for *this* bird:

1. **Palette.** Spectra 6 blue is a saturated primary blue. The heron's slate blue-grey,
   rusty-brown thighs and soft orange dagger have no native matches — they'd dither or
   substitute, and dithering breaks the flat-shape storybook law the art was locked under.
2. **Tempo.** At 12–28 s per frame there is no strike, no flight — a fishing event becomes
   a slow page-turn of keyframes, minutes long. What e-ink *does* suit is the project's own
   stillness law: the bird stands motionless most of its life, and an unpowered still bird
   on a wall is genuinely magical.
3. **Price ceiling.** Waveshare already sells a finished, wood-framed Spectra 6 photo
   product at ~€101 — a DIY e-ink heron at $99–149 would sit right on top of it.

**Prototype BOM, e-ink route:** panel $45–62 + driver board ~$15–25 + frame/case $6–10 +
power $5 ≈ **$71–102**, for the slower, palette-compromised version.

## Status: DECIDED by Jimmy 2026-11-02 — 800×480 on ESP32-S3

Jimmy picked the S3 route over the P4 in the main chat; the model grid was retargeted
to 160×96 (exact ×5) mid-build. What this brief still adds: the **named board and
buy-ready BOM** for when the sim preview lands and a parts order gets its go
(no parts bought yet, per Jimmy's sequencing — v1 sim first).

- **Board to buy when the word comes:** Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-4.3B (or its -BOX
  cased variant if a printed case isn't wanted for the prototype). US$29.99 launch
  price; ~$37–44 at EU/UK retailers, ~$25–33 marketplace, at check time 2026-11-02.
  US stock/price to be re-verified at order time — not yet checked on Amazon/Waveshare US.
- **Case design inputs, already known:** board outline 112.4 × 75.1 mm, ~17–18 mm thick;
  USB-C on board; touch is capacitive, so the printed frame must not cover the panel face.
- **P4 / 1024×600 stays the documented presence upgrade** (~$20/unit more, grid redesign
  + retrain, 7" / 164 × 99 mm) if a bigger second edition is ever wanted.
- **E-ink is dead by Jimmy's own ruling** (refresh killed motion) — do not revive it.

Price anchors on the local shelf: Arduboy candle $149 (Jimmy owns one), Inky Frame 7.3"
$99.75 (static images only). A fluid, locally-printed heron at $99–149 is not fantasy pricing.
