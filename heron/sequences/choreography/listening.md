# LISTENING — Choreography Spec (REPLACES Miss, Jimmy 2026-11-05 23:30)

*Design capture only — no drawing yet. Playback 12 fps. Jimmy's concept, verbatim

listening. it will be a short animation up to that pose. a long hold, and
finally settling back down to starting — so 2 consecutive clips. neck up and
body postured, and neck down and body back to original position." Hold length
set by Jimmy 23:31: the pose is held on a SINGLE FRAME for 15 SECONDS.*

## Structure — a pair, always back-to-back (like Flight Out / Flight Home)

1. **CLIP 1 — RISE** (short, ~3 s / ~36 ticks): exact home → the listening pose.
   Beats: the body gathers and postures up; the neck UNFOLDS upward (neck law:
   it unfolds, never lengthens) to a steep, near-vertical angle; the head comes
   high; a last small settle into the tall stance. Final frame = THE POSE.
2. **THE HOLD — 15.0 s (180 ticks) on the single pose frame.** Stored once;
   the player holds the still. No repeated frames, no storage cost.
3. **CLIP 2 — SETTLE** (short, ~3 s / ~36 ticks): THE POSE → exact home.
   The neck folds back down, the body relaxes and sinks back to the original
   hunting posture; final frame is the exact home drawing.

Total behavior ≈ 21 s, of which 15 s is the one held frame.

## The pose
The visual opposite of home: home is the deep, low hunting coil; LISTENING is
the tallest posture in the set — neck extended up at a steep angle, head high,
body stood tall. Feet stay planted in the home stance throughout (no travel,
no side change, waterline fixed, rings only at the feet, whole bird in frame,
marsh unchanged).
