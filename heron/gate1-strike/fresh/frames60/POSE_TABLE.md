# POSE TABLE — Sequence 1 (Strike), 60 frames (crew order 2026-11-05 16:10)

One unique drawing per tick, t0–t59, 12 fps, 5.0 s. Drawings ONLY — no
animation artifacts. Production runs in APPROVAL-GATED batches of 5 ticks
(t0–4, t5–9, …, t55–59); nothing in a later batch is drawn before the GO.

## Coordinates and anchors
- Canvas 2048×1152. Bill-tip targets in 0–1000 normalized coords (x = % of
  width ×10, y = % of height ×10), the same system as
  fresh/DENSIFY_STATUS.md and the redraw's CHAIN_SPEC.
- HOME tip (472,848). HOME = fresh/home_frame_approval.png (LOCKED): t00 is
  the home file itself (raw copy = K_coil_color_redo.png; registered copy =
  home_frame_approval.png verbatim); t59 is the home file again.
- Steps: every consecutive step ≤ ~45 units EXCEPT the two marked physical
  snaps (t10 launch release, t17 peak-exit). Steps ≤ ~35 preferred.
- Legs PLANTED in every frame (sequence-1 law): front foot raw x ≈ 16.5% of
  width, rear ≈ 23%, waterline y ≈ 96.5%, small ripple rings at each foot in
  EVERY frame. Whole bird inside the frame. Frill whole.
- Water arc: rings born at the touch point at t15, droplet at t17 exit,
  spread → wide/faint → ghost → calm. Blank paper only.
- Color: every drawing born in the home register (home attached to every
  generation call). Gate: bird_sat ≥ 42.9 and never paler/bluer than home on
  view. No post-hoc color work.

Sources: v2/redraw phase skeleton (freeze t0–9, extension t10–15, peak beat
t15–16, withdrawal t17–43, settle t44–59); bill-tip anchors from
DENSIFY_STATUS.md + redraw CHAIN_SPEC targets, re-spaced so each tick
advances in small even steps; the redraw's nine failed slots (A6, A8, MIDW,
B4, B7, D0, D1, E1, E5) carry explicit targets here — they are drawn, not
absorbed. f = withdrawal fraction (t−17)/40 for water states.

## Method (binding)
- Keys (marked KEY) drawn first within each batch and gated before the
  batch's breakdowns; breakdowns chained at most one step from a key or a
  verified same-batch neighbour.
- Anti-clone gate: measured tip strictly advancing along the table's path
  (between neighbours, ≥15% of the interval) except the starred rows.
- Escalation is mandatory: 2 failed edit attempts on a slot → fresh FULL
  drawing from its pose row; up to 6 total attempts per slot; a slot still
  failing at 6 is reported as a GAP — never absorbed as a hold.
- Registration: uniform transform scale 0.80, offset (+48,+204) via
  fresh/build_v2.py register(); registered feet target 15.2% / 20.3% ± 0.8.
